UW SEAL

Low Power Magnetic Linear Actuator

Low-power camera positioning actuator using a 3-phase coil drive, alternating-polarity magnet array, Ansys Maxwell FEA, and ESP32-based wireless motion control.

Final project poster summarizing system requirements, magnetic design targets, ESP32 control architecture, simulations, and measured final results.

Integrated camera-cart prototype on linear rails, showing the magnet array, payload platform, and mechanical test setup for bidirectional positioning.

Capstone showcase presentation with the project team, featuring the completed low-power magnetic linear actuator prototype and final poster.

Washington Nanofabrication Facility

Microfabrication Project

PECVD oxide deposition, photolithography, RIE etching, Cr/Al and Al/Cu metallization, oxide uniformity validation, and diode IV/CV electrical failure analysis.

Final patterned wafer after the WNF microfabrication process, including lithography, etching, and metal deposition steps.

Schottky diode electrical characterization using probe-station imaging and IV measurement to evaluate device behavior.

Cleanroom process waiting period between tool runs while maintaining wafer handling protocol.

ABM Contact Aligner used for contact photolithography exposure and mask-to-wafer pattern transfer.

Rite Track automated coater cluster for photoresist coating, HMDS vapor priming, edge-bead removal, and bake steps.

Barrel Etcher used for isotropic oxygen plasma processing, including resist stripping, descum, and organic residue removal.

Reactive Ion Etcher 2, a parallel-plate RIE system used for plasma-based anisotropic etching.

Manual benchtop spin coater with vacuum chucking for small samples through wafer-scale resist coating.

AZ726 development step used to reveal photoresist patterns after UV exposure.

HMDS oven used for wafer surface priming to improve photoresist adhesion before coating.

SPTS PECVD system used for plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition of dielectric thin films.

Four Dimensions Model 280 four-point probe used for sheet resistivity measurements on processed samples.
